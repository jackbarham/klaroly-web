# Desktop and app booking software for wedding makeup artists

*Lede:* Do your admin at your desk. Keep it up to date from your phone.

The proper sit-down work happens on a laptop, with a keyboard and a cup of tea. The rest of it happens standing up: a quote on the way to a trial, a note between clients, a date pencilled in while you are holding a brush.

**On the phone, it is tap, tap, done.** At the desk, it is everything else. Same information in both.

**We are building it now, with professional artists.**

## The bit nobody mentions at makeup school

A wedding makeup artist runs a small business on a spreadsheet, a notes app, a calendar, a contract template that gets copied and pasted, and about four hundred unread Instagram messages. It works, right up until two weddings land on the same Saturday in June and nobody notices until March.

The admin is not hard. It is just endless, and it happens in the gaps: on a train, between clients, at half past ten at night.

## What Klaroly does about it

- **Capture all your enquiries.** An enquiry comes in, you tap it in, it goes on your calendar as a soft hold. So when the next one asks about that Saturday, you already know.
- **Bookings that hold everything.** Names, venue, trial date, wedding date, call time, what was asked for, what was agreed, what has been paid and what has not.
- **Quotes from your own rate card.** Set your prices once. Bride, bridesmaids, mother of the bride, children, gents, travel, early start, hotel night. Adjust any of them on any booking when the job calls for it.
- **Contracts that get signed.** A proper agreement sent as a link. They read it, type their name, tick the box, sign. No printing, no scanning, no photograph of a signature taken at an angle.
- **Invoices, deposits and balances.** Deposit now, balance before the day. Klaroly knows what is outstanding and chases it so you do not have to write that message again.
- **Notes you can actually find.** Skin type, sensitivities, reference photos, what went well at the trial, what the mother of the bride said. Attached to the booking, not scattered across three apps.

## Built for a 5am call time

Weddings start early and venues have terrible signal. Klaroly keeps the day on your phone whether or not there is any reception: addresses, call times, notes, the lot.

## Who it is for

Wedding and bridal makeup artists in the UK, whether that is forty weddings a year or four. Hair stylists too, and anyone else who takes a booking for a date and travels to it. If you would like to stop writing the same email, this is aimed at you.

We are starting with wedding makeup deliberately, because building for everybody at once is how software ends up suiting nobody.

## Be one of the first

We are talking to working artists now about what matters and what does not. If you would like a say in that, or you just want to know when it opens, leave your email.

# What Klaroly does

*Lede:* A tour of the parts. Everything here is being built now, and the order is being set by the artists we are talking to.

## Enquiries

Most bookings start as a message read while your hands are full. Klaroly gives enquiries their own place instead of leaving them in an inbox.

- Add an enquiry in a few taps: name, date, venue, how they found you
- It goes on the calendar as a soft hold straight away
- Clash warnings cover enquiries, not just confirmed bookings, so you know a date is getting busy before you promise it to anybody
- Turn an enquiry into a booking without typing any of it again

## Bookings and the calendar

One booking holds the whole job.

- The client, their contact details, and anybody else involved
- Trial date and wedding date, with times and call times
- The venue, with travel time and distance worked out for you
- What was agreed, what it costs, what has been paid
- List view or calendar view, whichever suits the moment

## Quotes and your rate card

Set your prices once and stop working them out on a calculator.

- A line for every service you offer: bride, bridesmaids, mother of the bride, children, gents, whatever you call yours
- Travel, early starts and hotel nights as their own lines, because they are their own costs
- Build a quote on your phone in a few taps and send it before you have got back to the car
- Override any price on any booking when a job is not standard
- Prices are recorded on the booking as they were agreed, so raising your rates next year does not quietly rewrite last year

## Contracts

The part most artists put off.

- A proper agreement, with your terms and the booking details filled in
- Sent as a link, nothing to download or install
- They read it, type their full name, tick the box, submit
- Both sides get a signed PDF immediately, and a confirmation email goes out automatically
- A full record of when it was sent, opened and signed, kept in case it is ever needed

## Invoices, deposits and balances

- Deposit on booking, balance before the day, which is how most artists already work
- Generate an invoice from the booking, no re-typing
- Klaroly tracks what is outstanding and how close the date is
- Reminders go out on their own until it is paid, and stop when it is
- Record a bank transfer in one tap, because most clients will pay that way
- Agreed to wait? Snooze the reminders without pretending the invoice is paid

## Emails that send themselves

- Enquiry acknowledgements, booking confirmations, trial reminders, balance chasers
- Written once, in your words, then left to run
- Everything sent is logged against the booking, so you can see what went out and when

## Client notes and photos

- Notes as a dated stream, so you can see how a booking developed
- Skin type, sensitivities, the look they want
- Trial photos and wedding photos at full resolution, kept with the booking
- Add a reminder to any note

## On your phone, and at your desk

Klaroly is one product with three faces: an iPhone app, an Android app, and a desktop version in the browser. The same information in all three.

The phone is for the ninety per cent that is tap, tap, done. The desktop is for setting up your templates and doing your month end, which nobody wants to do with their thumbs.

## What is coming next

We are building the booking, contract and invoice side first. After that:

- **Card payments.** Take a deposit or a balance by card, straight from the invoice.
- **Deeper automations.** More of the messages you send by hand, sent for you.
- **Hair, and the rest of the wedding morning.** Other people who turn up with a car full of kit have close to the same problem.

Early access members get a say in the order.

## What we are deliberately not doing

- **Not a directory.** Klaroly does not send you clients and does not rank you against other artists. It is your tool, not a marketplace.
- **Not a form builder.** You will never be asked to design a form. Sections turn on and off, that is all.
- **Not something your clients log into.** They get a link when they need one. Nobody is asked to create an account to book a face.

# Why we are building this

*Lede:* Klaroly started with a professional makeup artist complaining about her admin to her brother-in-law.

The artist is [Jess](https://www.instagram.com/jessicabmakeupartist/). The brother-in-law is [Jack](https://www.jackbarham.com), who builds software for a living. The complaint was not about the work, because the work is the good bit. It was about the twelve places a booking lives before it becomes a wedding: the Instagram message, the email, the note in the phone, the calendar entry, the contract template that never quite gets updated, the invoice, the reminder that did not get sent.

There is software for this. Most of it was built for salons, where the client comes to you and books a forty-minute slot. A wedding is a different shape entirely. It is booked eighteen months out, it involves five people, it happens somewhere you have to drive to at five in the morning, and the money arrives in two pieces months apart. Bending salon software around that is possible. It is just not much fun.

So we decided to build something for the actual shape of the job.

## Who is behind it

Klaroly is made by **Sunday South**, a small UK studio that has been building web and mobile products for other people for fifteen years. This one is ours.

**Not funded by anybody.** No investors, no growth targets, no pressure to add features nobody asked for. It is paid for by the people who use it, which keeps the incentives simple.

## Are you a hair or makeup artist?

If you handle weddings or on-site bookings, we want to hear from you. Help us finish this off, and shape the features you want most. That is not a polite line. The order things get built in is being set by the artists we are talking to.
